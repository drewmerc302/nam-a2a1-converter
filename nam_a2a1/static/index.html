NAM A2 → A1 Converter

Distill NAM A2 captures into A1 .nam files that A1-only devices (e.g. the Valeton GP-5 / GP-50 / GP-150) accept. Drop an A2, get an A1.

1. A2 captures

Drag & drop .nam A2 captures here, or

    • Draft (20) — quick preview. Rough, artifacts likely; use only to sanity-check a file.
    • Standard (60) — the sweet spot. Faithful tone at a sane runtime. Default.
    • Best (120) — chasing the last few %. Diminishing returns and noticeably longer.

    More epochs = longer convert but tighter tone. Runtimes are far shorter on a GPU — Apple silicon uses it automatically; on Windows and Linux it needs the CUDA build. On an older CPU expect tens of minutes even at Draft. The progress bar shows an estimate once training starts.
